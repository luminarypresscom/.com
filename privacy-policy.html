Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy describes exactly what Luminary Press collects through this website, luminarypress.online, and nothing more. We don't run ads, sell data, or use tracking cookies.

Who We Are

Luminary Press ("we," "us") is a book marketing and author promotion service. This policy covers the personal information we collect when you visit or interact with luminarypress.online.

Information We Collect

We collect information only when you choose to provide it, through:

  • The contact form: your name, email address, and message, plus a book title if you provide one.
  • The on-site chat widget: your name and email address, along with any messages you type into the chat.
  • Direct email: if you email us directly, we receive whatever information you include in that message.

We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, or advertising pixels on this site, and we do not collect any information automatically beyond what your browser sends as part of normal web traffic (such as to our hosting provider).

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide the services you've requested.
  • To send you the information, quotes, or materials you've asked for (such as a free book growth blueprint).
  • To improve our services based on the questions and feedback we receive.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

To operate this site, we rely on a small number of third-party services, each of which processes limited data on our behalf:

  • EmailJS: used to deliver messages from our contact form and chat widget directly to our inbox. When you submit a message, your name, email, and message content pass through EmailJS's infrastructure to reach us.
  • Google Fonts: used to load the typefaces on this site. Loading a font may involve your browser making a request to Google's servers.

We don't control these third parties' own data practices beyond what's needed to deliver our site and messages; you can review EmailJS's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy directly.

Data Retention

We retain messages you send us for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain business records, typically no longer than is useful for ongoing client relationships. You can request deletion at any time (see below).

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of the personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, email us at sam@luminarypress.online and we'll respond promptly.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for authors, publishers, and business inquiries. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how we collect or use information, we'll update this page and revise the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at sam@luminarypress.online.
